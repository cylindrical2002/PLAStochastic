## Detailed Mathematical Statements

### 1. `B-MOM`: Brownian Affine Lifetime Moment Recurrences

Let $(B_t)_{t \ge 0}$ be a standard one-dimensional Brownian motion ($B_0 = 0$, $\mathbb{E}[B_t^2] = t$). On $[0, t]$, let $D_{\mathrm{opt}}(B, t) = \inf_{a, b \in \mathbb{R}} \sup_{0 \le s \le t} |B_s - a s - b|$ denote the optimal uniform affine approximation error and $D_{\mathrm{an}}(B, t) = \inf_{a \in \mathbb{R}} \sup_{0 \le s \le t} |B_s - a s|$ denote the origin-anchored uniform approximation error. Their first-exit lifetimes are $\tau_A = \inf\{t > 0 : D_A(B, t) > 1\}$ for $A \in \{\mathrm{opt}, \mathrm{an}\}$.

All positive integer moments $m_{q, A} = \mathbb{E}[\tau_A^q]$ ($q \ge 1$) are rational linear combinations of $\zeta(2j+1)/\pi^{2j}$ ($1 \le j \le q$) generated by the two-step polynomial recurrences

$$
U_0(x) = 1, \quad U_1(x) = x, \quad U_{n+2}(x) = 2x U_{n+1}(x) - (x^2 - x) U_n(x),
$$

$$
V_0(x) = 1, \quad V_1(x) = 3x, \quad V_{q+2}(x) = 2x V_{q+1}(x) - (x^2 + 4x) V_q(x).
$$

In particular, $\kappa_{\mathrm{opt}} = \mathbb{E}[\tau_{\mathrm{opt}}] = \frac{40}{\pi^2}\zeta(3) - \frac{4}{3}$, $\mathbb{E}[\tau_{\mathrm{opt}}^2] = \frac{2240}{\pi^4}\zeta(5) - \frac{224}{3\pi^2}\zeta(3)$, and $\alpha_{\mathrm{opt}} = \operatorname{Var}(\tau_{\mathrm{opt}}) / \kappa_{\mathrm{opt}}^2 > 0$.

### 2. `S-SCALE` & `S-BRACKET`: Native Swing Restart Asymptotics and Parity Brackets

For a standard Gaussian random walk with tolerance $E \to \infty$ under the native Swing filter (constrained-SSE slope selection with carry-point restart and no `max_lag`), let $L_{k, E}$ denote the length of the $k$-th completed segment:

$$
\mathbb{E}[L_{2, E}] \sim \kappa_2 E, \qquad \mathbb{E}[L_{k, E}] \sim c_k E \log E \quad (k \ge 3),
$$

where $\rho$ is the unique limiting law of the first restart margin, $\alpha_2 = \rho \mathsf{A}$, $\kappa_2 = 2 \int_0^\infty a^{-1} \alpha_2(da) > 0$, and $c_k = \alpha_2 \mathsf{T}^{k-3} h > 0$. Moreover, if $\lambda$ denotes the unique invariant slope law ($\lambda \mathsf{T} = \lambda$) and $\gamma_{\mathrm{Swing}} = \lambda h$, the transient coefficients satisfy the alternating parity brackets

$$
c_3 \ge c_5 \ge c_7 \ge \cdots \downarrow \gamma_{\mathrm{Swing}}, \qquad c_4 \le c_6 \le c_8 \le \cdots \uparrow \gamma_{\mathrm{Swing}},
$$

with geometric contraction $|c_k - \gamma_{\mathrm{Swing}}| \le L_h W_1(\alpha_2, \lambda) (3/4)^{k-3}$ for all $k \ge 3$.

### 3. `P-GEP`, `P-KEP`, & `P-CEPS`: Endpoint Promotion Height Laws and Fanout Expansion

Let $H_{\mathrm{EP}}$ denote the exact hierarchy height of optimal greedy segmentation (`ALG-OPT`) under Endpoint Promotion (EP), and let $C_\varepsilon = \lim_{j \to \infty} C_\varepsilon(\Phi_\varepsilon^j \operatorname{Exp}(1))$ denote the exact stationary single-layer compression factor:

- **`P-GEP` (Model G)**: For every i.i.d. positive gap law $\nu_0 \in L^3$ with positive mean and variance, there exist $\varepsilon_0(\nu_0), K_{\nu_0} > 0$ such that $|\mathbb{E}[H_{\mathrm{EP}}] - \log n / \log C_\varepsilon| \le K_{\nu_0}$ for all $n \ge 2$ and $\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon_0(\nu_0)$, with no growth restriction on $n$ relative to $\varepsilon$.
- **`P-KEP` (Model K)**: For every bounded, strictly positive Lipschitz density $f$ on $[A, B]$, there exist $\varepsilon_0(f), K_f > 0$ such that $|\mathbb{E}[H_{\mathrm{EP}}] - \log N / \log C_\varepsilon| \le K_f$ for all $N \ge 2$ and $\varepsilon \ge \varepsilon_0(f)$, governed by the exact same denominator $\log C_\varepsilon$.
- **`P-CEPS` (Asymptotic Series)**: There exists a unique sequence $(a_j)_{j \ge 0}$ of real coefficients such that for every fixed integer $M \ge 0$, $C_\varepsilon / \varepsilon^2 = \sum_{j=0}^M a_j \varepsilon^{-j} + O_M(\varepsilon^{-M-1})$ as $\varepsilon \to \infty$, with leading constant $a_0 = \kappa_{\mathrm{opt}} / \alpha_{\mathrm{opt}}$.

### 4. `P-GRE` & `P-KRE`: Rank-Equispaced Promotion Height Laws

Let $H_{\mathrm{RE}}$ denote the hierarchy height of `ALG-OPT` under complete-coverage Rank-Equispaced Promotion (RE) with rank stride $d_\ell = \lfloor N_\ell / M_{\ell+1} \rfloor$:

- **`P-GRE` (Model G)**: Along $n, \varepsilon \to \infty$ with $\log \varepsilon = o(\log n)$, let $A = \kappa_{\mathrm{opt}} \varepsilon^2 / v_0$ and $y_n = \log_2(\frac{\log n}{\log A} + 1)$. Outside rounding windows of width $w_n = K(1/\log \varepsilon + \log\log n / \log n)$ around $\mathbb{Z}$, $H_{\mathrm{RE}} = \lceil y_n \rceil$ with probability $1 - O(n^{-2} + (\log n)^{-2})$, and $\mathbb{E}[H_{\mathrm{RE}}] - \log_2\log_\varepsilon n \in [-1, 0] + o(1)$.
- **`P-KRE` (Model K)**: Uniformly over $\delta \in [0, 1]$ and $f \in \mathfrak{F}_\delta$ on $[0, 1]$, $H_{\mathrm{RE}} = \Phi(N, \varepsilon, \delta) + O(1)$ with probability $1 - O(N^{-2})$ and in expectation, where $\Phi(N, \varepsilon, \delta) = \log_2 \frac{\log N}{\log \varepsilon} + \log_2^+ \frac{\log N}{\log(\varepsilon/\delta)}$. For any fixed non-constant density $f$, let $z_N = \log_2(\frac{\log N}{6\log \varepsilon} + \frac{1}{2})$ and $h_K(z) = 2\lceil z \rceil + 1 + \mathbf{1}\{\lceil z \rceil - z < \log_2(3/2)\}$; outside width-$w_N$ neighbourhoods of $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z} - \log_2(3/2)$, $\mathbb{P}(H_{\mathrm{RE}} \ne h_K(z_N)) \le 63 / N^2$ and $|\mathbb{E}[H_{\mathrm{RE}}] - h_K(z_N)| \le 200 \log N / N^2$.
